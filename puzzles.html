How It Works

The Daily Puzzle

  • One puzzle a day in each room, the same for everyone. It changes at midnight on your device.
  • The week climbs: Monday to Thursday can be solved with the basic rules alone; the weekend asks for more.
  • Your first finish of the day is the one kept, with your streak of days in a row. Play again as often as you like.
  • Learn to Play is in every room: a short lesson and practice puzzles that never touch your streak.
