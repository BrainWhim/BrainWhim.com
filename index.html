About BrainWhim

BrainWhim is a small studio for imagination and everyday life.

We build tools that help people catch ideas and actually live them — across learning, daily rhythms, play, and making. The four tracks (Learn, Live, Play, Create) are equal on purpose. Real life doesn’t stay in one lane, and the tools shouldn’t either.

Some of what we’re building is light and playful. Some of it is more grounded: systems for mindful living, routines, and recovery support when people need steadier footing. The goal is the same either way — turn raw imagination into something useful, human, and lasting.

More apps are on the way. This site is the front door.
