About the Studio

About BrainWhim

BrainWhim is a small studio in Georgia making apps, games and daily tools for curious people. Everything we build lives in one of four pillars: Learn (short lessons that stick), Live (habits and routines), Play (games and puzzles worth your time) and Create (aids for writers, makers and designers).

Play is open now. The other wings open one room at a time.

How the House Works

Visitor, House Guest, Club Member

  • VisitorTry any game or today's puzzles on this device. No account needed.
  • House GuestA free account: unlimited games, online play, points and streaks saved.
  • Club MemberEvery table and board, Club avatars, the Streak Shield, more points a day.

Questions or something broken? Write to support@brainwhim.com. We read every note.
