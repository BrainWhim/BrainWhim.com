Avatars

Drawn in code on your galaxy, so they stay sharp at any size. Tap any avatar to make it "yours"; the rank ladder, charms and table examples below change to match. Ranks and charms are earned by everyone, free or Club, and they decorate your avatar rather than replacing it.

Choose Your Face

The Families

Three free families and two Club families. Every avatar sits on its own patch of the Lobby's galaxy.

Earned · Board Games

The Rank Ladder

One ladder for all ten board-game rooms, shown as a ring around your avatar. Cards will get a ladder of its own later. The point levels are only a suggestion.

Earned · One per Room

Game Charms

A small charm hangs on your ring for each room you master (for example, beating The House at level 10). You choose which one to wear. Ten rooms, ten charms; six are shown.
